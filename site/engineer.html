Alphonso Woodbury · New York, NY

I build the platforms other teams build on.

Software and data/AI platform engineer. I take ambiguous, large-scale problems and turn them into systems people rely on — from the streaming pipeline to the model to the interface, and from the architecture diagram to the executive who has to sign off on it.

Data & AI platforms Batch + streaming · ML/RL/AI Multi-cloud End to end

For six years I've built the unglamorous, load-bearing parts of large systems — the data movement, the platforms, the internal tools — at a global bank, as a founding engineer twice over. The throughline of my career is simple: make the people downstream faster, and keep the quality of what flows through them high.

Then I built one of my own, in the open: a complete, working miniature of a modern data/AI platform. Not a contribution to one — the whole vertical slice, from a live event stream to an evaluated model. It's the proof behind everything below.

How I work

Five things I'm consistent about.

Ownership

Own the whole problem

Ingestion to interface. I don't hand off the hard part or stop at the boundary of my box — I take the outcome, not the ticket.

Leverage

Build leverage, not just features

My best work is the platform that makes the next ten things easy — self-serve, declarative, config-driven. One system that retires a hundred one-offs.

Judgment

Make the tradeoff explicit

Cheap-and-elegant vs industry-standard. Managed vs self-hosted. Build vs buy. I've run both sides across the market, so I choose deliberately — and can defend it.

Discipline

Cost and reliability are design

Not afterthoughts. I've run a full platform at near-zero cost and operated it live. I design for the bill and the pager from the first diagram.

Clarity

Communicate so anyone can decide

An engineer and a CFO should both leave the conversation knowing what to do, and why. I write the doc people actually use to make the call.

What I've built

Two platforms, one pattern.

01 At JPMorgan Chase
The data that runs the firm — and the tools to use it

Founding engineer on the platform that brings external data into the bank: configuration-driven onboarding that balanced data quality against maintainability as sources multiplied, handling files up to 384GB without turning each new source into a project. Then the internal tool on top — a self-serve marketplace that made 150,000+ datasets searchable and usable for technical and non-technical teams, serving 2M+ daily queries.

02 RPX — in the open
A modern data/AI platform, end to end

The whole vertical slice, built solo and public: streaming ingestion (Kafka/Flink, stateful, run live), a batch lakehouse (Spark/Airflow/BigQuery/dbt), human-preference data, the full breadth of models — ML, RL, computer vision, forecasting, and a provider-agnostic LLM agent — plus evaluation, observability, and safety. Across five clouds, on free tiers, at a $0/month target.
