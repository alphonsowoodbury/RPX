# Car 3D Models — Grab List

Models for the "Garage" section (cars I've owned). These sites gate downloads behind
a free account + license click, so they can't be auto-fetched — log in, download, and
drop the file into the matching folder below.

For free Sketchfab models: click **Download 3D Model** (top right), pick glTF/glb or
the format you want, and accept the CC-BY attribution (credit the author on the site).
